Overview

A tax audit is an examination of accounts to ensure compliance with the provisions of the Income Tax Act. It helps verify the accuracy of financial records and tax filings.

Why Tax Audit is Important

Tax audits promote transparency, prevent fraud, and ensure businesses meet statutory obligations. They also help identify areas for financial improvement and compliance risks.

Process

We conduct thorough audits of books of accounts, financial statements, and tax records. Our team ensures compliance with audit requirements and provides actionable insights.
